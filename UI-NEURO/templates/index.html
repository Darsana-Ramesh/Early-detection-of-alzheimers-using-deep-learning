AI-Powered Brain Health Analysis

NeuroVista uses advanced Graph Neural Networks to analyze brain scan data and detect early signs of Alzheimer's disease. Our system helps identify risks at an early stage for better clinical decisions.

Multi-View Brain Analysis

We examine brain activity from multiple perspectives — axial and coronal — to detect subtle changes in communication between brain regions. This improves accuracy and early detection.

Detailed Brain Region Tracking

Our system studies 116 important AAL atlas brain regions to identify areas linked to memory, thinking, and cognitive health, highlighting the top supporting regions for every prediction.

Risk Progression Assessment

For patients with Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI), we estimate whether the condition is stable or may progress to Alzheimer's, helping guide early medical intervention.
